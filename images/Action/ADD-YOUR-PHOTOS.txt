ACTION PHOTO SLIDESHOW

Upload real JPEG photos to images/action/ using these exact filenames:
action-1.jpg through action-30.jpg

You can upload any number up to 30. Missing files are skipped automatically.
Photos appear in numerical order. Filenames are lowercase and use .jpg.
Renaming a PNG or HEIC file to .jpg does not convert it; export it as JPEG first.
Landscape photos around 1600 pixels wide work well.
If no action photos load, the existing images/players.jpeg is used.

Replace index.html once with this version. Afterwards, just upload photos to the folder and refresh the website.
For more than 30 slots, change length:30 in ACTION_PHOTOS in index.html.
